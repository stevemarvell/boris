Expansion switches
The switch sits on the sibship line at the point the branch is elided — so it reads as “the line continues here”, not as a control parked beside the diagram. That placement is what lets the same control mean “expand these siblings” in one place and “expand this family” in another.

Expansion is local
Only the subtree under that switch relaids out. Sibling branches elsewhere in the generation do not move, so the reader never loses the person they were looking at.
Two things the switch never does
It does not re-centre the view, and it does not change which person is focused. Both are what tapping a chip does — keeping them separate is what makes the diagram explorable rather than jumpy.
State belongs to the view, not the data
Which branches are open is view state. It is not derived from the family and is not stored with it — re-centring on someone else starts collapsed again.
