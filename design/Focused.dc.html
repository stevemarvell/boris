Rank, not radius
Parents sit above, children below, spouse beside, siblings on the same rank to the left. Generation stays vertical, so this view and the overview teach the same reading direction.
Siblings are dimmed
They are the one group reachable two ways — as siblings here and as children of the couple above. Dimming says “same rank, not the subject” without drawing a second set of lines.
What is missing on purpose
No cousins, no grandparents. They are one more tap away. Drawing them here is what makes a focused view degenerate back into the overview.
