Width is the only thing that changes
1 → 2 → 4 → 8 → 44 people. Each step relaids out one subtree and leaves the rest alone, so the person under the reader's finger stays under it.
Where to start
Step 2 is the right default for a fresh open — a couple explains itself, a lone chip does not. Step 1 exists only as the result of a search.
What step 5 tells you
Fully open is a legitimate state and a poor default. It earns a control of its own — expand all, then zoom to fit — rather than being somewhere you arrive by tapping fifteen times.
