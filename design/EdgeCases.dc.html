The rule underneath. Absence is drawn as absence. A missing spouse removes the bar, missing children remove the drop and the bus, a single child collapses the bus to nothing. No placeholders and no dotted ghosts for people who are not there.
Where the ring goes. Only on a line that genuinely ends — someone with no children. It is not a badge for being unmarried, which is why Olivia and Peter carry one and Nora carries one, but for different halves of the same reason.
