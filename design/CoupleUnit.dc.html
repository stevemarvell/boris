1
A couple never splits. The two chips and their bar are one box. Layout moves the box; it never places one partner without the other.
2
The blood relative sits left, the married-in partner right (dashed). Descent always reads down the left edge, so the spine stays straight however many partners join.
3
Children set the width. A unit is as wide as its children need; the couple centres over that span. Nothing downstream reflows a parent.
4
Each sibling group gets its own bus line at a fixed offset below the couple. Two buses never share a y, so crossing lines cannot be mistaken for a relationship.
5
Cousin branches reserve their own column band. Subtrees claim disjoint horizontal space, so no two families interleave — the reason the wide view scrolls rather than overlaps.
6
Degenerate cases collapse, they do not special-case. No spouse means no bar. No children means no drop. One child means a bus of zero length. See the edge-case artboard.
Why this is the primitive
The data stores only parents and spouse. Children and siblings are derived, so the drawing has to be too — a unit is computable from one couple plus “everyone whose parents are these two”, with no extra state and nothing to keep in sync.
